<objective>
Fix the crash triggered by the Monthly / By category toggle in the Sats earned & spent chart by adding a fixed lower bound and right-side padding to the category row chart's X axis.

Purpose: Without a fixed lower bound, LiveCharts auto-scales the axis over a collapsed range, generates a step size near zero, and enumerates more than 10,000 separators, crashing the app. The padding mirrors the working ExpensesByCategory / IncomeByCategory charts and leaves room for data labels.
Output: `BtcDenominatedMetricsChartData.cs` updated; solution builds; BTC metrics tests pass.
</objective>
