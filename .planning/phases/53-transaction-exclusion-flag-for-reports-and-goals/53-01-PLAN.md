<objective>
Tracer slice for Phase 53: the `IsExcluded` flag exists end-to-end on the write/read path — domain aggregate → LiteDB entity (additive BSON field, no migration) → all 12 entity↔domain mapping converters → CQRS command/DTO surface → the first report consumer (MonthlyTotalsReport income/expense buckets). Once this slice is green, the remaining reports/goals filters (plans 02/03) and the UI/MCP surface (plan 04) expand from a proven foundation.

Purpose: A per-transaction exclusion flag is the phase's core data model change; proving persistence and one real consumer before touching 10+ calculators avoids the Pitfall-4 failure mode (flag silently dropped at one of 12 mapping sites) reaching the report layer undetected.
Output: Flag persisted and round-tripped for all 6 transaction types; commands/DTOs carry it; MonthlyTotalsReport honors it in income/expense only.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions (no CONTEXT.md / discuss-phase output; research recommendations adopted)

- **D-P53-1 (semantic boundary):** exclusion affects income/expense report buckets and ALL goal progress/contribution calculations — never account balances, net-worth, ATH, max-BTC-stack, avg-price, wealth snapshots, or account totals.
- **D-P53-2 (schema gate):** LiteDB is schemaless — additive `[BsonField("excl")]` with missing-field-defaults-false replaces a migration script (precedent: Phase 29 `AssetEntity.IsSold`).
- **D-P53-3 (assumption-delta):** `gsd_run query assumption-delta scan 53 --json` → `detected: false` — **no-change**: the noun stays `Transaction`; an optional additive boolean does not promote the aggregate.
- **D-P53-4 (spec-less probe fallback):** skipped visibly — phase has no requirement IDs; must_haves are goal-derived.
- **D-P53-5 (event reuse):** `SetExcluded` reuses `TransactionEditedEvent` — MarkGoalsStaleEventHandler gives goal stale-marking for free; `UpdateAccountTotalEventHandler` must NOT subscribe (balances move regardless).
