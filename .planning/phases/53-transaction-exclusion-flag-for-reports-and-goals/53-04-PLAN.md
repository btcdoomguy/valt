## Recorded decisions

- **D-P53-4a (copy behavior):** copying a transaction RESETS the flag to false (research Q4 recommendation: a copy is a new economic event).
- **D-P53-4b (installments):** when adding a debt with installments, the flag copies to every installment transaction (existing installment loop copies all form fields).
- **D-P53-9 (MCP scope):** `excluded = false` optional param on the 6 Add* tools + one `SetTransactionExcluded(transactionId, excluded)` tool backed by a new `SetTransactionExclusionCommand` (cleaner than forcing full-detail EditTransactionCommand). Every mutating tool publishes `McpDataChangedNotification` (existing pattern).
- **D-P53-10 (localization key):** `ManageTransactions.ExcludedFromReports` → Designer property `ManageTransactions_ExcludedFromReports`; copy: EN "Excluded from reports and goals" / pt-BR "Excluído de relatórios e metas" / es "Excluido de informes y metas".
